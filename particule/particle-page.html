About this project

A real-time 3D particle engine controlled entirely by hand gestures. 3 000 particles self-organise into 8 procedural shapes — heart, galaxy, DNA double helix, torus, flower, sphere, nebula and tree — rendered at 60 fps via WebGL with Three.js.

MediaPipe Hands detects 21 landmarks per hand at ~25 fps and translates them into intuitive controls. A unique draw mode lets you trace any shape with your index finger — the particles then morph to match your custom drawing in real time.

  • 8 procedural shapes: Heart, Galaxy, Flower, Sphere, DNA, Torus, Nebula, Tree
  • Open hand (5 fingers) → real-time 3D rotation with X/Y axis lock
  • 3-finger pinch → smooth zoom with momentum & fist-freeze
  • Index finger at edge → cycle shapes left / right
  • Pinky gesture → draw mode: trace with index, validate with OK
  • Adaptive cursor smoothing — fast when moving fast, silky when slow
  • 3 000 particles, additive blending, capped at 1.5× device pixel ratio
